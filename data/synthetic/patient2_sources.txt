[D1] Discharge instructions, page 1, Medicines: Start rivaroxaban 10 mg once daily in the evening for 12 days (last dose October 26) to help prevent blood clots. Oxycodone 5 mg every 6 hours as needed for severe pain. Acetaminophen 1,000 mg (two 500 mg tablets) every 8 hours; do not take more than 3,000 mg in 24 hours. Docusate 100 mg twice daily while taking oxycodone. Continue metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals. STOP meloxicam while taking rivaroxaban.
[D2] Discharge instructions, page 1, Pain and safety: Do not take ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin), naproxen (Aleve), meloxicam (Mobic), or aspirin while taking rivaroxaban unless your surgeon tells you to. These raise the risk of bleeding. Do not drive or drink alcohol while taking oxycodone.
[D3] Discharge instructions, page 2, Incision care: Keep the dressing clean, dry, and covered for 5 days after surgery. You may shower after day 5. No baths, pools, or hot tubs until your surgeon says it is OK.
[D4] Discharge instructions, page 2, When to call: Call the orthopedic nurse line for a fever over 101°F (38.3°C); redness, warmth, or drainage at the incision; new or increasing swelling, pain, or warmth in either calf; unusual bruising, nosebleeds, or bleeding gums; or no bowel movement for 3 days.
[D5] Discharge instructions, page 2, Emergencies: Call 911 for chest pain or sudden shortness of breath, coughing up blood, signs of stroke, or bleeding that will not stop.
[D6] Discharge instructions, page 3, Follow-up: Physical therapy starts October 16, three times a week. Dr. K. Osei, October 28 at 9:00 a.m., for a wound check and staple removal. See your primary care provider within 2 weeks.
[L1] Rivaroxaban label (summarized for testing): Do not stop taking it without talking to your doctor; stopping early raises the risk of blood clots. It raises the risk of bleeding; report unusual bruising or bleeding. If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember on the same day; do not take two doses at the same time.
[L2] Oxycodone label (summarized for testing): Can cause drowsiness, dizziness, constipation, and slowed breathing. Do not drink alcohol. Take only as prescribed; taking more than prescribed can cause serious harm. Can lead to addiction.
[L3] Acetaminophen Drug Facts (summarized for testing): Severe liver damage may occur if you take more than the maximum daily amount, take it with other products containing acetaminophen, or drink 3 or more alcoholic drinks a day while using it.
[L4] Docusate Drug Facts (summarized for testing): Stool softener. Drink plenty of fluids. Stop use and ask a doctor if you have rectal bleeding or no bowel movement after use.
[L5] Metformin label (summarized for testing): Take with meals to reduce stomach upset.
